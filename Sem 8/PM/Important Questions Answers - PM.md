### 6. Project Termination – 10 Marks

**Q. (Repeated in Dec 2023, May 2023, May 2024)**  
*List and briefly describe the ways a project may be terminated. What are some non-technical reasons for project termination?*  

**Ways:**  
- Termination by Extinction (successful completion or failure)  
- Termination by Addition (becomes internal operation)  
- Termination by Integration (absorbed into parent org)  
- Termination by Starvation (budget cuts)  

**Non-technical reasons:**  
- Political changes  
- Loss of champion/sponsor  
- Strategic priority shift  
- Legal/regulatory issues  
- Morale problems

### 10. Goldratt's Critical Chain Method – 5 to 10 Marks

**Q. (Dec 2023, May 2023, Dec 2025)**  
*What is Goldratt's critical chain method?*  
→ Focuses on resource constraints, not just task dependencies. Uses:  
- Project buffer (at end of critical chain)  
- Feeding buffers (before non-critical chains merge)  
- Resource buffers (alert for resource availability)  
- Eliminates multi-tasking.

## ⚡ Pro Tip for Numericals

**In the exam, write these steps explicitly:**
1. Formula
2. Substitution with values
3. Calculation (show at least one term expanded)
4. Final answer with units
5. Decision/Interpretation

For NPV: *"Since NPV is positive/negative, the project should be accepted/rejected."*  
For EVM: *"CPI < 1 means over budget, SPI < 1 means behind schedule."*

Good luck! These cover ~60-70 marks directly.
